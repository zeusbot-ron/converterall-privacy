Apexion One for iPhone and iPad

Privacy Policy

Effective July 10, 2026 · Last updated July 10, 2026

Plain-language summary: Apexion One converts files locally on your device. The developer does not collect your files, conversion contents, prompts, or personal information. The public App Store build has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDK.

1. Scope

This policy applies only to the Apexion One iOS and iPadOS app (bundle identifier ai.openclaw.apexion). It does not describe other apps or services with similar ownership or branding.

2. Files and conversions

Files you choose are used only to perform the conversion you requested. Conversion work and output generation occur on your device. Apexion One does not upload your selected files or converted output to an Apexion One server.

3. Data the developer collects

The developer does not collect personal data through Apexion One. The app does not require an account and does not use IDFA, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. The public App Store build does not transmit conversion-feedback reports.

4. On-device search and AI

Conversion search uses built-in keyword matching and, when supported and available, on-device model capabilities such as Apple Foundation Models or a local model runtime. Search prompts are not sent to an Apexion One server.

5. Microphone and speech recognition

If you tap the microphone button, Apexion One asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission so you can dictate a conversion request. The app does not send microphone audio or transcripts to the developer. Apple may process speech recognition on-device or through Apple services depending on device, language, and system availability; that processing is governed by Apple’s privacy terms. You can deny or revoke these permissions in iOS Settings and type requests instead.

6. Local storage and retention

The app may store preferences, favorites, recent conversion identifiers, and temporary output/cache files locally on your device. You can remove this local data by clearing app data where available or uninstalling the app. Files you save or share remain wherever you choose to place them.

7. Apple platform services

Apple may process App Store activity, TestFlight activity, diagnostics, backups, and other platform information under Apple’s own policies. The developer does not receive your file contents through those services.

8. Children, sale, and sharing

Apexion One does not knowingly collect children’s personal information. The developer does not sell personal data and does not share personal data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated date will appear above. Questions can be sent to zeusbot@icloud.com.
