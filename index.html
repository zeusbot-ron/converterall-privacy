Privacy Policy

Effective July 14, 2026 · Last updated September 7, 2026

Plain-language summary: ConverterAll converts files locally on your device. The developer does not collect your files, conversion contents, prompts, or personal information. The public App Store build has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDK.

1. Scope

This policy applies only to the ConverterAll iOS and iPadOS app. It does not describe other apps or services with similar ownership or branding.

2. Files and conversions

Files you choose are used only to perform the conversion you requested. Conversion work and output generation occur on your device. ConverterAll does not upload your selected files or converted output to a ConverterAll server.

3. Data the developer collects

The developer does not collect personal data through ConverterAll. The app does not require an account and does not use IDFA, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. The public App Store build does not transmit conversion-feedback reports.

4. On-device search and AI

Conversion search uses built-in keyword matching and, when supported and available, on-device model capabilities such as Apple Foundation Models or a local model runtime. Search prompts are not sent to a ConverterAll server.

5. Camera and file selection

ConverterAll does not request microphone or speech-recognition permission. When an iOS file picker offers Take Photo and you explicitly choose it, iOS may request camera permission so the photo can be added directly to the conversion. ConverterAll does not send camera images to the developer, and you can choose an existing file instead.

6. Local storage and retention

The app may store preferences, favorites, and recent conversion identifiers locally on your device. After an explicit Save or Share action, a temporary output copy is written to the app-private iOS cache and deleted when the native share sheet closes, including its error path. Files you save or share remain wherever you choose to place them. You can remove remaining local preferences by clearing app data where available or uninstalling the app.

7. Apple platform services

Apple may process App Store activity, TestFlight activity, diagnostics, backups, and other platform information under Apple’s own policies. The developer does not receive your file contents through those services.

8. Children, sale, and sharing

ConverterAll does not knowingly collect children’s personal information. The developer does not sell personal data and does not share personal data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Terms of Use

By using ConverterAll, you agree to use it lawfully and only with files you own or are authorized to process. Conversion results can vary by file format, file quality, device capabilities, and operating-system support. Review important output before relying on it.

ConverterAll is provided “as is” without warranties to the maximum extent permitted by law. The developer is not responsible for lost data, incompatible output, or indirect damages resulting from use of the app. Keep backups of important files. These terms do not limit rights that cannot legally be limited.

The app is currently offered free of charge. Apple’s standard App Store terms also apply.

Support

ConverterAll does not require an account. For help, include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, the conversion name, input format, and a description of what happened. Do not email confidential files or passwords.
